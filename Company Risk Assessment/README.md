

# Company Cybersecurity Risk Assessment

## Overview

This project demonstrates a complete cybersecurity risk assessment for a fictional cloud-based SaaS company using Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) principles.

The assessment identifies organizational assets, evaluates cybersecurity risks, recommends mitigation strategies, and maps controls to ISO/IEC 27001.

---

## Objectives

- Identify business assets
- Identify threats and vulnerabilities
- Perform risk assessment
- Develop a risk treatment plan
- Map controls to ISO/IEC 27001
- Produce executive-level documentation
