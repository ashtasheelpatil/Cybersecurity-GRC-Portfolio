# Internal Audit Plan

## Purpose
This workbook defines a risk-based internal audit plan for the simulated ABC Cloud Technologies Ltd. ISMS.

## Audit Flow
**Plan → Opening Meeting → Evidence Collection → Sampling → Evaluate Evidence → Record Findings → Closing Meeting → Corrective Action → Follow-up**

## Contents
- Audit scope, criteria and objectives
- Five-day audit schedule
- Process/control coverage
- Auditors and auditees
- Audit methods
- Expected evidence
- Risk focus
- Sampling plan
- Auditor checklist
- Audit guidance

## ISO 27001 Lead Auditor Skills Demonstrated
Risk-based planning, defining criteria and objectives, evidence-based auditing, sampling, independence, finding classification and corrective-action follow-up.

## Important
All dates, people, evidence, findings and organization details are simulated for portfolio purposes.
