# Asset Inventory

## Purpose
The Asset Inventory identifies information assets, applications, infrastructure, services, endpoints, and other resources that may be exposed to information security risk.

The inventory provides the foundation for the risk assessment.

## Source
The assets were identified from the simulated ABC Cloud Technologies Ltd. Company Profile.

## Classification Approach
Assets are classified as:

- **Critical** — Loss or compromise could severely disrupt core services, expose highly sensitive information, or create major business impact.
- **High** — Loss or compromise could significantly affect operations, customers, compliance, or reputation.
- **Medium** — Loss or compromise would create a manageable but material impact.
- **Low** — Loss or compromise would have limited business consequences.

## CIA Assessment
Each asset is assessed for:

- **Confidentiality** — importance of preventing unauthorized disclosure.
- **Integrity** — importance of preventing unauthorized alteration or destruction.
- **Availability** — importance of maintaining access when required.
