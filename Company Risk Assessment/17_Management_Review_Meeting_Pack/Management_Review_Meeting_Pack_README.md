# File 17 — Management Review Meeting Pack

## Purpose
A management-facing pack for reviewing the suitability, adequacy and effectiveness of the simulated ISMS.

## Workbook sheets
1. **Management Review** — meeting metadata, review inputs and required management outputs.
2. **ISMS KPIs** — security/GRC performance indicators and management actions.
3. **Risk Summary** — key risks and residual-risk treatment status.
4. **Audit Findings** — internal audit findings and management decisions.
5. **Management Action Plan** — actions approved during the review.
6. **Minutes & Decisions** — meeting decisions, owners and due dates.
7. **Instructions** — how to prepare, conduct and document the review.

## Simulated management decisions
- Prioritize the privileged-access/MFA finding.
- Complete critical-workload restore testing.
- Strengthen logging and monitoring evidence.
- Standardize secure-development evidence.
- Improve ISMS KPI reporting and evidence consistency.

## Lead Auditor interview point
Management review is not simply a meeting. The auditor should look for documented inputs, decisions, actions, assigned responsibilities, due dates and evidence of follow-up.
