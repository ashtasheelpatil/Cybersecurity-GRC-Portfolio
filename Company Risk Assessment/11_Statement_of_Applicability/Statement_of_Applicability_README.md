# Statement of Applicability

## Purpose
This workbook provides a portfolio-style Statement of Applicability (SoA) aligned to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A.

## Traceability
**Risk → Risk Treatment → Annex A Control → Applicability → Implementation Status → Evidence**

## What is included
The workbook contains 25 selected Annex A controls relevant to the simulated organization, including:
- Access control
- Identity management
- Authentication information
- Cloud service security
- Protection of records
- Security awareness
- Remote working
- Privileged access
- Source-code access
- Malware protection
- Technical vulnerability management
- Configuration management
- Backup
- Logging
- Monitoring
- Secure coding
- Environment separation
- Change management
- Incident management
- Business continuity
- Compliance

## Important ISO 27001 Concept
The Statement of Applicability records the controls the organization has determined necessary, whether they are implemented, and the justification for their inclusion or exclusion.

It is not sufficient to mark controls as applicable without a defensible connection to organizational context, risks, requirements, and treatment decisions.

## Portfolio Limitation
This is a simulated SoA for a GRC portfolio. It is not evidence that ABC Cloud Technologies Ltd. is ISO/IEC 27001 certified or that the listed controls have actually been implemented.
