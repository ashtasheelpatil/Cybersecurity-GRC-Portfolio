# TPRM Scope

## In Scope
- Cloud service providers
- SaaS providers
- Payment processors
- Data processors
- IT service providers
- Managed security providers
- Consultants and contractors with system/data access
- Suppliers supporting critical business processes

## Out of Scope
Low-risk purchases with no access to organizational information, systems, facilities, or critical services may use a simplified procurement process.

## Risk Domains
- Information security
- Privacy
- Operational resilience
- Business continuity
- Compliance
- Financial viability
- Concentration and fourth-party risk
- Physical security
- Reputational risk
