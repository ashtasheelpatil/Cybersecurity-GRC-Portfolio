# Project Charter — Third-Party Risk Management

## Project
Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) Program

## Organization
NovaFin Technologies LLC (fictional)

## Objective
Establish a structured lifecycle to identify, assess, treat, monitor, and offboard third parties that may introduce information security, privacy, operational, compliance, or business risks.

## Scope
All external suppliers, vendors, service providers, contractors, cloud providers, and other third parties that access organizational information, systems, facilities, or services.

## Key Outcomes
- Maintain an authoritative third-party inventory.
- Apply risk-based due diligence.
- Document risk treatment and acceptance.
- Embed security requirements into contracts.
- Monitor critical suppliers.
- Manage third-party incidents.
- Perform periodic reassessments and secure offboarding.

## Success Criteria
- 100% of active third parties recorded.
- Critical vendors risk assessed before onboarding.
- High and critical risks have documented treatment.
- Required contracts contain applicable security clauses.
- Critical vendors are periodically reviewed.
