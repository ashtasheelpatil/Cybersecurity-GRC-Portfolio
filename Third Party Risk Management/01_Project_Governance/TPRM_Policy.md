# Third-Party Risk Management Policy

## 1. Purpose
This policy establishes requirements for managing risks arising from third-party relationships.

## 2. Principles
- Risk-based decision making
- Security and privacy by design
- Least privilege
- Due diligence before onboarding
- Contractual accountability
- Continuous monitoring
- Documented risk acceptance
- Secure termination

## 3. Requirements
Third parties shall be classified according to criticality and risk. Appropriate due diligence shall be completed before material access or service delivery begins. Security, privacy, continuity, incident notification, and audit requirements shall be incorporated into contracts where applicable.

## 4. Exceptions
Exceptions require documented risk assessment, compensating controls where appropriate, business owner approval, and defined expiry/review dates.

## 5. Review
The policy should be reviewed at least annually and after material changes to the TPRM program.
