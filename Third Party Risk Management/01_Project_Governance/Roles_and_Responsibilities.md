# Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Board / Executive Management | Oversight and risk acceptance for material risks |
| TPRM Manager | Owns the TPRM lifecycle |
| Procurement | Coordinates supplier engagement and commercial records |
| Information Security | Performs security due diligence and control assessment |
| Privacy / Legal | Reviews privacy, regulatory and contractual requirements |
| Business Owner | Defines service criticality and accepts business risk |
| IT | Validates technical architecture and access requirements |
| Internal Audit | Independently evaluates TPRM controls |
| Vendor | Provides evidence, remediation and notifications |

No vendor should be onboarded solely on the basis of procurement approval where material security or privacy risk exists.
