# Vendor Approval Workflow

1. Business owner submits vendor request.
2. Procurement records the supplier.
3. Vendor is categorized.
4. Required due diligence is performed.
5. Risk score is calculated.
6. Security/privacy/legal reviews are completed as applicable.
7. Risks are treated or formally accepted.
8. Contract requirements are confirmed.
9. Appropriate authority approves onboarding.
10. Vendor is added to the active inventory.
11. Monitoring schedule is established.
