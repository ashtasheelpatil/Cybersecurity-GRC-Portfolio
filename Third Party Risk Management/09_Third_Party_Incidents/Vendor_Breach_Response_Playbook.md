# Third-Party Breach Response Playbook

## 1. Detect
Identify and validate the supplier incident.

## 2. Notify
Confirm contractual and regulatory notification requirements.

## 3. Contain
Coordinate containment with the vendor and internal teams.

## 4. Assess
Determine affected systems, data, customers, and business services.

## 5. Remediate
Track corrective actions and security improvements.

## 6. Recover
Restore normal service and validate controls.

## 7. Review
Perform root-cause analysis and update the vendor risk assessment.

## Evidence
Maintain incident communications, timelines, reports, decisions, and remediation evidence.
