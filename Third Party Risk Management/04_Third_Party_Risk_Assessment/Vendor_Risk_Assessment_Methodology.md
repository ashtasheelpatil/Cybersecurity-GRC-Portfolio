# Vendor Risk Assessment Methodology

## Scoring
Likelihood and impact are scored from 1 to 5.

Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact

| Score | Rating |
|---:|---|
| 1–4 | Low |
| 5–9 | Medium |
| 10–16 | High |
| 17–25 | Critical |

## Impact Factors
Consider confidentiality, integrity, availability, regulatory impact, financial impact, customer impact, and reputational impact.

## Assessment Triggers
- New vendor onboarding
- Material service change
- Significant incident
- Major control deficiency
- Contract renewal
- Periodic reassessment
- Regulatory change
