# Privacy Due Diligence Checklist

- [ ] Identify categories of personal data.
- [ ] Identify processing purposes.
- [ ] Identify processing locations.
- [ ] Confirm lawful processing responsibilities.
- [ ] Review data retention requirements.
- [ ] Review sub-processors.
- [ ] Confirm data subject support processes.
- [ ] Review security measures.
- [ ] Confirm breach notification obligations.
- [ ] Confirm deletion/return requirements at termination.
- [ ] Review cross-border transfer mechanisms where applicable.
