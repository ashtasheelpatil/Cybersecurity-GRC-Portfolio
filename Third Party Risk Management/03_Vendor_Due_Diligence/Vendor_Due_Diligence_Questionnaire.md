# Vendor Due Diligence Questionnaire

## Organization
- Legal entity and ownership
- Primary locations
- Key contacts
- Subcontractors / fourth parties

## Security
- Information security program
- ISO/IEC 27001 or equivalent certification
- SOC reports where applicable
- Vulnerability management
- Penetration testing
- Encryption
- Identity and access management
- Logging and monitoring
- Secure development lifecycle

## Privacy
- Personal data processed
- Processing purposes
- Data locations
- Retention
- Sub-processors
- Data deletion
- Privacy incident notification

## Resilience
- Business continuity
- Disaster recovery
- Recovery objectives
- Backup strategy
- Testing frequency

## Incident Management
- Incident response process
- Notification timelines
- Customer communication process
- Forensic support

## Evidence
All responses should be supported by appropriate evidence where risk warrants it.
