# TPRM Audit Report

## Audit Objective
Evaluate whether the TPRM lifecycle is designed and operating effectively.

## Scope
Vendor inventory, due diligence, risk assessment, contracts, monitoring, incidents, and offboarding.

## Sample Findings
### Finding 1 — Medium
Evidence of privileged-access reviews was incomplete for one high-risk vendor.

### Finding 2 — Medium
One vendor's sub-processor register required updating.

### Finding 3 — Observation
Monitoring evidence was stored inconsistently across procurement and security repositories.

## Overall Conclusion
The TPRM program is established and generally operating, with opportunities to improve evidence consistency and remediation tracking.
