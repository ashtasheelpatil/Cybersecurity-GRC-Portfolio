# TPRM Audit Checklist

- [ ] Approved TPRM policy exists.
- [ ] Third-party inventory is maintained.
- [ ] Critical vendors are identified.
- [ ] Due diligence is risk-based.
- [ ] Security/privacy evidence is retained.
- [ ] Risk assessments are documented.
- [ ] Risk acceptance is approved.
- [ ] Contracts contain applicable security requirements.
- [ ] Vendors are monitored.
- [ ] Incidents are tracked.
- [ ] Findings are remediated.
- [ ] Offboarding is controlled.
