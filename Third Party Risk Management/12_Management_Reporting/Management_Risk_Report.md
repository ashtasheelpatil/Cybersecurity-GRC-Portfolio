# Third-Party Risk Management — Management Risk Report

## Executive Summary
The TPRM program covers six active vendors, including two critical vendors. Critical suppliers have documented risk assessments and assurance evidence. Several medium-level actions remain open.

## Key Risks
1. Cloud resilience and availability.
2. Payment security.
3. Email privileged access.
4. Employee data processing.
5. Contractual evidence consistency.

## Management Actions
- Ensure remediation deadlines are tracked.
- Require current evidence for critical vendors.
- Strengthen centralized evidence management.
- Review contractual security gaps during renewal.

## Overall Risk Posture
**Moderate**, with no currently identified critical residual risk.
