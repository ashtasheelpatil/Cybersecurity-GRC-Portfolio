# TPRM Management Review Pack

## Agenda
1. TPRM program performance
2. Critical vendor status
3. High-risk vendor remediation
4. Vendor incidents
5. Audit findings
6. Contract/security gaps
7. Emerging supplier risks
8. Decisions and actions

## Required Decisions
- Approve risk treatment priorities.
- Approve material risk acceptance where applicable.
- Resource remediation of overdue findings.
- Confirm monitoring requirements for critical suppliers.
