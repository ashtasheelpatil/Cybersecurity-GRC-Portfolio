# Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM)

A practical, portfolio-ready Third-Party Risk Management project for a fictional fintech organization, NovaFin Technologies LLC.

## Lifecycle
Vendor Identification → Categorization → Due Diligence → Risk Assessment → Risk Treatment → Contract Controls → Approval → Monitoring → Incident Management → Audit → Offboarding → Management Reporting

## Repository Structure
1. Project Governance
2. Third-Party Inventory
3. Vendor Due Diligence
4. Third-Party Risk Assessment
5. Risk Treatment
6. Contracts and Security Requirements
7. Vendor Approval
8. Ongoing Monitoring
9. Third-Party Incidents
10. Audit and Compliance
11. Offboarding
12. Management Reporting

## Framework Alignment
The project is designed to demonstrate practical concepts aligned with ISO/IEC 27001/27002, ISO 31000, NIST Cybersecurity Framework, and NIST SP 800-161 supply-chain risk management concepts.

## Important Note
This is a fictional portfolio project. Vendor names, records, evidence, risk ratings, and scenarios are illustrative and should not be treated as real assurance evidence.

## Suggested Portfolio Statement
"Designed and documented a complete Third-Party Risk Management lifecycle covering vendor inventory, risk-based due diligence, vendor risk assessment, contractual security requirements, monitoring, incident management, audit, and secure offboarding."
