# Vendor Categorization

## Critical
A vendor is critical when disruption, compromise, or failure could materially affect critical business services, regulated data, financial processing, or organizational resilience.

## High
Significant operational, security, privacy, or compliance impact is possible.

## Medium
Limited but meaningful business or information-security impact.

## Low
Minimal impact and no material access to systems, facilities, or sensitive information.

Classification should be reassessed when services, data, access, ownership, or threat exposure changes.
