# Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Incident Manager | Coordinates response and incident record |
| Security Analyst | Detection, triage and analysis |
| IT/Engineering | Containment, eradication and recovery |
| Legal/Privacy | Regulatory and privacy assessment |
| GRC/Compliance | Evidence, compliance mapping and corrective actions |
| Management | Risk and business decisions |
| Communications | Approved internal/external communications |
