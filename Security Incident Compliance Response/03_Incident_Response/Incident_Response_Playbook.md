# Incident Response Playbook

## Preparation
Maintain contacts, procedures, logging, backups and response capabilities.

## Detection & Analysis
Validate the event, establish scope, identify indicators and classify severity.

## Containment
Isolate affected accounts, endpoints, workloads or segments while preserving evidence.

## Eradication
Remove malicious artifacts, close exploited weaknesses and reset compromised credentials.

## Recovery
Restore trusted systems, validate services and monitor for recurrence.

## Post-Incident
Complete root cause, compliance assessment, corrective actions and lessons learned.
