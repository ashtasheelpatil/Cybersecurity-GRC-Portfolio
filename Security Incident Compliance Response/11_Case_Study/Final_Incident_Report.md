# Final Incident Report

## Executive Summary
A simulated ransomware/data-breach incident resulted in unauthorized access and disruption of selected systems.

## Root Cause
Credential compromise through phishing, amplified by identity, privilege, detection and segmentation weaknesses.

## Response
Affected assets were contained, evidence preserved, compliance obligations assessed, malicious activity eradicated and services recovered.

## Outcome
Corrective actions were assigned and incorporated into continual improvement.
