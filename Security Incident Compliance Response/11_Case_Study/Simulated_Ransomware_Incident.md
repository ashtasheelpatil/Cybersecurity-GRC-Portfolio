# Simulated Ransomware Incident

**Day 1:** Phishing leads to credential compromise.

**Day 2:** Suspicious authentication is detected; unauthorized access is confirmed.

**Day 2:** Lateral movement is identified and systems are isolated.

**Day 3:** Ransomware disrupts selected systems.

**Day 3–4:** Credentials are revoked, evidence preserved and containment strengthened.

**Day 4+:** Recovery, compliance assessment and notification decision proceed.

**Post-Incident:** Root cause, corrective actions, effectiveness review and management review are completed.
