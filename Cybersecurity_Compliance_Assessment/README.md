# Cybersecurity Compliance Assessment

## Project Overview
A practical, portfolio-ready cybersecurity compliance assessment project designed to demonstrate how an organisation can assess its information-security governance, risks, controls, compliance status, audit readiness, and continual improvement.

## Primary Frameworks
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- ISO/IEC 27002:2022
- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF)
- Risk management principles aligned with ISO 31000
- Applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, and organisational requirements

## Project Objectives
1. Establish the assessment scope and organisational context.
2. Identify information-security risks and compliance obligations.
3. Assess the design and implementation of security controls.
4. Identify compliance gaps and nonconformities.
5. Develop risk treatment and corrective-action plans.
6. Produce audit-ready evidence and management reporting.
7. Establish continual-improvement activities.

## Suggested Assessment Lifecycle
**Plan → Identify → Assess → Treat → Audit → Review → Improve**

## Assessment Rating
- **Compliant** – requirement is met and evidence is sufficient.
- **Partially Compliant** – requirement is partly met or evidence is incomplete.
- **Non-Compliant** – requirement is not adequately implemented.
- **Not Applicable** – requirement is demonstrably outside the assessment scope.

## Risk Rating
Use a 5 × 5 model:
- Likelihood: 1–5
- Impact: 1–5
- Risk Score = Likelihood × Impact
- 1–4 Low
- 5–9 Medium
- 10–16 High
- 17–25 Critical

## Important Note
This is a portfolio/practice assessment framework. Organisation-specific legal and regulatory requirements, evidence, scope, risk appetite, and control applicability must be validated before using it for a real compliance decision.
