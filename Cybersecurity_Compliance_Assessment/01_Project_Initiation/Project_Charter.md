# Project Charter

## Project Title
Cybersecurity Compliance Assessment

## Purpose
Assess the organisation's cybersecurity governance, information-security controls, risk management, and compliance posture against defined requirements.

## Objectives
- Determine current compliance maturity.
- Identify control gaps and risks.
- Evaluate evidence supporting control implementation.
- Prioritise remediation.
- Report results to management.

## Scope
Information, people, processes, facilities, applications, infrastructure, cloud services, third parties, and security controls that support the defined business processes.

## Key Stakeholders
- Executive Management
- Information Security / GRC
- IT Operations
- HR
- Legal / Compliance
- Business Process Owners
- Internal Audit
- Third-Party/Vendor Management

## Deliverables
1. Assessment plan
2. Risk register
3. Compliance assessment
4. Control assessment
5. Gap/finding register
6. Risk treatment plan
7. Management report
8. Continual improvement register
