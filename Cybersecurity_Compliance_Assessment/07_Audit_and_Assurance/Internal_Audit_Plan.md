# Internal Audit Plan

## Audit Objective
Determine whether the information-security management system and selected controls conform to defined requirements and are effectively implemented.

## Audit Activities
- Opening meeting
- Document review
- Interviews
- Evidence sampling
- Control testing
- Finding validation
- Closing meeting
- Audit reporting
- Corrective-action follow-up

## Finding Classification
- Major Nonconformity
- Minor Nonconformity
- Observation
- Opportunity for Improvement
