# AI Incident Response Playbook

## Examples
- Harmful or unsafe AI output
- Material bias/discrimination issue
- Privacy or confidential-data exposure
- Security compromise or prompt injection
- Significant model degradation
- Unauthorized AI use
- Third-party AI outage or material change

## Response
1. Detect and log
2. Triage severity
3. Contain
4. Preserve evidence
5. Assess impact
6. Notify required stakeholders
7. Remediate
8. Validate recovery
9. Perform root-cause analysis
10. Track corrective actions and lessons learned
