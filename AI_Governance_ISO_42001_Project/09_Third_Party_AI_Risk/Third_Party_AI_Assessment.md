# Third-Party AI Risk Assessment

## Supplier Due Diligence
Assess:
- AI system purpose and limitations
- Security controls
- Data handling and retention
- Subprocessors
- Model training/use of customer data
- Availability and resilience
- Incident notification
- Transparency and documentation
- Change notification
- Audit/assurance evidence
- Contractual and regulatory obligations

## Approval
High-risk AI suppliers require enhanced due diligence and documented business/security approval before production use.
