# AI Lifecycle Governance Procedure

## 1. Idea and Intake
Register the use case and identify intended purpose, users, data, provider, and expected outcomes.

## 2. Risk and Impact Assessment
Complete proportionate AI risk and impact assessments before approval.

## 3. Design and Development
Define requirements, data controls, validation criteria, security requirements, and human oversight.

## 4. Testing and Validation
Test accuracy, robustness, security, bias/fairness where relevant, and failure modes.

## 5. Approval and Deployment
Document approvals, operating constraints, monitoring requirements, and rollback/retirement criteria.

## 6. Monitoring
Monitor performance, incidents, complaints, drift, misuse, security events, and material changes.

## 7. Change Management
Reassess when the purpose, model, data, provider, risk profile, or operating environment materially changes.

## 8. Retirement
Disable access, retain required records, revoke credentials, and document retirement decisions.
