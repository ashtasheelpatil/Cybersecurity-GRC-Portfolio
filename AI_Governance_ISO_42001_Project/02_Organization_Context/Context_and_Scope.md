# Organization Context and AIMS Scope

## Internal Issues
- AI adoption across business functions
- Limited AI governance maturity
- Dependence on cloud and third-party AI providers
- Need for consistent risk assessment
- Data quality and privacy considerations

## External Issues
- Regulatory expectations
- Customer contractual requirements
- Rapid changes in AI technology
- Model and supplier concentration risk
- Public expectations around trustworthy AI

## Interested Parties
- Customers
- Employees
- Management
- Regulators
- Suppliers
- Data subjects / affected individuals
- Business partners
- Investors

## Proposed AIMS Scope
Governance and lifecycle management of AI systems that are developed, procured, deployed, or operated by ApexAI Solutions within its business and customer-facing services.

## Exclusions
Any exclusion must be justified, documented, and reviewed for continued validity.
