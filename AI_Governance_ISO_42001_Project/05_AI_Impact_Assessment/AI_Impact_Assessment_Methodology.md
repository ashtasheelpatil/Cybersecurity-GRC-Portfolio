# AI Impact Assessment Methodology

Assess potential effects on individuals, groups, organizations, and society where relevant.

## Assessment Areas
1. Human rights and dignity
2. Safety and wellbeing
3. Fairness and discrimination
4. Privacy and data protection
5. Employment and economic effects
6. Accessibility
7. Transparency and user understanding
8. Security and misuse
9. Environmental considerations where material

## Outcome
Each assessment should document affected stakeholders, foreseeable harms, severity, likelihood, mitigation measures, residual impact, approval, and review date.
