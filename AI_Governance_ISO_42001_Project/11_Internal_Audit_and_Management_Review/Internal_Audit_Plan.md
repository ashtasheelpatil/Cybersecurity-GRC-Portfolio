# AI Management System Internal Audit Plan

## Audit Objectives
- Evaluate conformity with the AIMS requirements and internal arrangements.
- Evaluate whether controls are implemented and effective.
- Identify risks, nonconformities, and improvement opportunities.

## Audit Areas
- Governance and leadership
- Scope and context
- Risk and impact assessments
- AI inventory
- Lifecycle controls
- Data governance
- Supplier management
- Incident management
- Monitoring and measurement
- Continual improvement

## Evidence
Policies, registers, approvals, assessments, testing records, monitoring dashboards, supplier reviews, incidents, corrective actions, and management review records.
