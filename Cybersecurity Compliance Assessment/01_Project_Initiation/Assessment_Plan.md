# Cybersecurity Compliance Assessment Plan

## Assessment Approach
1. Confirm scope and criteria.
2. Identify applicable requirements.
3. Review documented information.
4. Interview responsible personnel.
5. Sample operational evidence.
6. Assess control design and implementation.
7. Record findings.
8. Agree remediation actions.
9. Report results.

## Assessment Criteria
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- ISO/IEC 27002:2022
- Applicable organisational policies
- Applicable legal/regulatory requirements
- Contractual and customer requirements
- Internal risk criteria

## Evidence Principles
Evidence should be relevant, sufficient, reliable, traceable, current, and attributable to the responsible control owner.
