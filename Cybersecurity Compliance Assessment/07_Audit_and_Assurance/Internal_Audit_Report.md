# Internal Audit Report

## Executive Summary
The assessment identified a generally established cybersecurity control environment with improvement opportunities in evidence consistency, access review governance, and change-management documentation.

## Positive Practices
- Security policies are established.
- Incident response planning is documented.
- Risk treatment activities are being tracked.

## Findings
### Finding 1 – Access Review Evidence
**Classification:** Minor Nonconformity / improvement subject to evidence validation.

### Finding 2 – Emergency Change Documentation
**Classification:** Observation / potential nonconformity subject to sampling.

## Conclusion
Management should prioritise remediation, verify effectiveness, and retain objective evidence of closure.
