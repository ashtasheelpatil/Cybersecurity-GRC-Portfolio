# AIMS Objectives and Metrics

## Suggested Objectives
- 100% of production AI systems inventoried.
- 100% of high-risk AI systems assessed before deployment.
- 100% of material AI incidents logged and investigated.
- Complete scheduled internal audits.
- Close corrective actions within agreed deadlines.

## Suggested KPIs
- AI risk assessment completion rate
- AI impact assessment completion rate
- AI incidents by severity
- Mean time to detect/respond
- Open corrective actions
- Supplier assessment completion
- Model monitoring coverage
- Training completion
