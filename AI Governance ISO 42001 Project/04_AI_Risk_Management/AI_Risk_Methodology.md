# AI Risk Management Methodology

## Risk Identification
Consider risks arising from:
- Data
- Models and algorithms
- Security
- Privacy
- Bias and discrimination
- Accuracy and reliability
- Human oversight
- Transparency
- Third parties
- Regulatory and contractual obligations
- Misuse and unintended use
- Operational resilience

## Scoring
Likelihood: 1–5  
Impact: 1–5  
Inherent Risk = Likelihood × Impact

Residual risk is calculated after controls and treatment actions are considered.

## Treatment
- Avoid
- Reduce
- Transfer/share
- Accept

High and critical residual risks require documented approval by the designated risk owner.
