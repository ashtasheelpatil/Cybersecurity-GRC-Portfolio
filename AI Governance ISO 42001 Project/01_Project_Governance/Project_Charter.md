# AI Management System Project Charter

**Organization:** ApexAI Solutions Pvt. Ltd.  
**Project:** AI Governance & ISO/IEC 42001 Implementation  
**Sponsor:** Chief Executive Officer  
**Project Owner:** AI Governance / GRC Manager

## Scope
Establish an AI Management System covering AI systems developed, procured, deployed, and operated by the organization.

## Deliverables
- AIMS scope
- AI governance policy
- AI risk methodology and risk register
- AI impact assessment process
- AI system inventory
- Control applicability/evidence register
- Data and AI lifecycle controls
- Third-party AI risk process
- Incident and nonconformity process
- Internal audit program
- Management review pack
- Continual improvement register

## Success Criteria
- Governance responsibilities assigned
- Material AI risks assessed and treated
- AI systems inventoried
- Applicable controls mapped and evidenced
- Audit findings tracked to closure
- Management review completed
