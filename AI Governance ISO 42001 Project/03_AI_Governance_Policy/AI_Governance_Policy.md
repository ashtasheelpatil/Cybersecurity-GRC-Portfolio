# AI Governance Policy

## Purpose
Establish principles and responsibilities for responsible development, procurement, deployment, use, monitoring, and retirement of AI systems.

## Principles
- Accountability
- Transparency and explainability appropriate to context
- Fairness and non-discrimination
- Human oversight
- Safety, security, robustness, and resilience
- Privacy and responsible data use
- Traceability and documentation
- Continuous monitoring and improvement

## Governance
The AI Governance Committee oversees material AI risks, approves high-impact use cases, reviews significant incidents, and monitors AIMS performance.

## Mandatory Requirements
AI systems must be inventoried, risk-assessed, and subject to proportionate controls before production use.
