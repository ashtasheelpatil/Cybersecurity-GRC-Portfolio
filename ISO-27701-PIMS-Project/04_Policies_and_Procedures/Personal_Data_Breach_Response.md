# Personal Data Breach Response
1. Report suspected loss, unauthorized disclosure, alteration, destruction, or access promptly.
2. Log and triage; record discovery time, systems, and known facts.
3. Contain proportionately and preserve evidence.
4. Involve Security, Privacy, Legal/Compliance, system owners, and management.
5. Assess data, volume, affected individuals, consequences, safeguards, and risk.
6. Authorized personnel decide required regulator, individual, client, or contractual notifications and deadlines.
7. Investigate root cause and remediate.
8. Document timeline, decisions, evidence, notification rationale, and actions.
9. Verify actions and record lessons learned.
Deadlines vary by law, role, and contract. Proposed procedure; not proof of tested capability.
