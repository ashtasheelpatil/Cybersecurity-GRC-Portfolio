# Privacy by Design Procedure
For new products, features, AI capabilities, data uses, vendors, or material changes: describe the change; identify subjects/data/flows; assess necessity and less intrusive alternatives; apply minimization and privacy-friendly defaults; assess risk and DPIA need; review transparency, rights, retention, security, vendors and transfers; assign actions; block launch for unresolved mandatory requirements or unacceptable risks; document approvals; verify controls before launch; monitor and reassess after changes. Keep design reviews, data flows, assessments, decisions, approvals, tests, and post-launch reviews.
