# Privacy Policy — Internal Draft
FinSecure (fictional) intends to process personal data fairly, transparently, for defined purposes and with appropriate safeguards.

**Principles:** define purposes and lawful basis; minimize collection; maintain accuracy; avoid unnecessary retention; apply risk-appropriate safeguards; support applicable rights; assess privacy implications of changes; maintain accountable records.

**Responsibilities:** management provides direction/resources; Privacy Lead coordinates the PIMS; process owners document activities and controls; IT/Security maintains safeguards; personnel follow procedures and escalate concerns.

**Third parties/incidents:** assess roles, necessity, security, contracts, subprocessors, transfers, retention, rights support, and exit. Suspected breaches must be promptly reported and assessed. Authorized personnel decide notifications under applicable law and contract. Draft only; not an approved operational policy.
