# Third-Party Privacy Procedure
Describe service, purpose, data, access, and need; determine provider role; tier risk by sensitivity, volume, access, location, subcontracting, and potential harm; perform proportionate privacy/security due diligence; review contract terms covering instructions, confidentiality, security, subprocessors, rights assistance, incidents, audits, transfers, and deletion; approve before sharing; apply least privilege; monitor changes/incidents/renewals; and manage exit/access revocation/deletion. Confirm provider locations and transfer safeguards where applicable.
