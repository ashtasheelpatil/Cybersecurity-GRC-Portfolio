# Data Subject Rights Request Procedure
1. Receive and log request with unique ID/date.
2. Acknowledge where appropriate and verify identity proportionately.
3. Clarify scope only when reasonably necessary.
4. Determine applicable law, role, exceptions, and deadline.
5. Search relevant systems and coordinate processors.
6. Review third-party rights, privilege, security, and restrictions.
7. Respond within applicable deadline or communicate a permitted extension.
8. Execute approved action where required.
9. Record communications, decisions, dates, and evidence in a restricted tracker.
10. Track request types, completion times, overdue cases, and root causes.
Deadlines and rights vary by law. Validate before operational use.
