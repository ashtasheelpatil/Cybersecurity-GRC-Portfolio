# PIMS Internal Audit Plan and Checklist
| Area | Objective | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Governance | Scope, roles, objectives reviewed | Scope, approvals |
| Inventory/RoPA | Records complete and traceable | Registers, validation |
| Rights | Requests and deadlines tracked | Redacted request log |
| Retention | Rules approved, disposal evidenced | Matrix, deletion records |
| Risk | Risks and treatment tracked | Register, action evidence |
| Vendors | Providers/contracts reviewed | Due diligence, contracts |
| Incidents | Escalation and decisions documented | Incident record/tabletop |
| Training | Awareness completed | Training report |
| Corrective action | Findings closed and verified | CAPA register |
| Management review | Risks/performance considered | Minutes, decisions |

Record criterion, sample, evidence, result, finding, risk, owner, and due date. Use “Not tested” where evidence was not examined.
