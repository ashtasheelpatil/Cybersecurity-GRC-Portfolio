# Privacy Objectives and Measurement Plan
| ID | Objective / metric | Proposed target | Owner | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PO-01 | Reviewed RoPA activities / total × 100 | ≥95% | Privacy Lead | Quarterly |
| PO-02 | Rights requests completed within applicable deadline / requests due × 100 | 100% | Privacy Lead | Monthly |
| PO-03 | Incidents triaged within internal target / incidents received × 100 | ≥95% | Security + Privacy | Quarterly |
| PO-04 | Staff completing assigned training / assigned staff × 100 | ≥95% | HR + Privacy | Quarterly |
| PO-05 | High-risk vendors reviewed / high-risk vendors in scope × 100 | 100% | Vendor Management | Quarterly |
| PO-06 | Systems with approved retention rules / systems in scope × 100 | 100% | Process Owners | Semiannual |
| PO-07 | Corrective actions closed on time / actions due × 100 | ≥90% | Control Owners | Monthly |
| PO-08 | High-risk new activities assessed before launch / total high-risk new activities × 100 | 100% | Product + Privacy | Quarterly |
| PO-09 | Access reviews completed / scheduled × 100 | 100% | IT/Security | Quarterly |
| PO-10 | Documents reviewed by due date / documents due × 100 | ≥95% | Document Owners | Quarterly |

Targets are illustrative. Define reporting periods, investigate misses, assign actions, and review targets with management. KPI measures performance; KRI signals changing exposure.
