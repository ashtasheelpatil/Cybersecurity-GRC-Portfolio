# Proposed PIMS Scope
The proposed PIMS covers governance and management of personal data processed by fictional FinSecure Technologies for its cloud financial workflow platform and supporting corporate functions, including onboarding, payment workflows, customer support, recruitment, employee administration, vendor management, and security monitoring.

**Functions:** Customer Operations, Payment Operations, Support, HR, Procurement, IT/Security, Product/Engineering, Legal/Compliance, and Privacy.  
**Information:** personal data, processing records, privacy requests, incident records, vendor records, assessments, and governance evidence.  
**Technology:** illustrative systems only; production assets are unknown.  
**Geography/third parties:** hosting locations, international transfers, and provider roles require confirmation.

The organization may act as controller for some activities and processor for others. No processing function is automatically excluded; any exclusion requires rationale, impact analysis, and approval. Review annually and after material changes. This is a proposed scope, not an approved operational scope.
