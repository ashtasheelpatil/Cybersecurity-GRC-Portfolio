# Interested Parties and Requirements
| Party | Needs/requirements to assess | Potential evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Customers/end users | Transparency, safeguards, rights handling | Notices, request tracker |
| Employees/applicants | Purpose limitation, access limits, retention | HR notices, access reviews |
| Business clients | Clear roles and contractual commitments | Contracts, DPAs |
| Regulators | Required records and accountable decisions | RoPA, assessments |
| Vendors/processors | Instructions, security, transfer/deletion terms | Due diligence, contracts |
| Management | Risk visibility, metrics, resources | KPI reports, minutes |
| Staff | Training and escalation routes | Training records, policies |
| IT/Security | Safeguards and incident coordination | Logs, access reviews |
| Legal/Compliance | Law and contract mapping | Legal review notes |
| Auditors | Traceable evidence | Audit records  |

Validate actual parties and requirements with process owners; this register is illustrative.
