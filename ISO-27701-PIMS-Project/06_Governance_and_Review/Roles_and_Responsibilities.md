# PIMS Roles and Responsibilities
| Role | Proposed responsibility |
|---|---|
| Executive Management | Policy, objectives, resources, material risk decisions |
| Privacy Lead | PIMS, inventory, RoPA, rights, risk, reporting |
| Process Owner | Purpose, record validation, operational controls |
| IT/Security | Safeguards, access, logging, incidents |
| Legal/Compliance | Applicable law, contracts, notifications |
| HR | Employee/applicant data and training |
| Vendor Management | Due diligence, contracts, reviews |
| Product/Engineering | Privacy in design, testing, release |
| Personnel | Follow policies and report concerns |
| Internal Auditor | Independent evidence-based assurance |
Assign actual role-holders in real use and avoid audit conflicts of interest.
