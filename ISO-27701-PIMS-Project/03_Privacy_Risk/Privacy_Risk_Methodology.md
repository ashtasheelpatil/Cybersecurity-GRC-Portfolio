# Privacy Risk Assessment Methodology
1. Define processing context, purposes, subjects, systems, recipients, and flows.
2. Identify plausible events, vulnerabilities, and harm to individuals.
3. Score likelihood and impact from 1–5; document controls and evidence.
4. Inherent risk = likelihood × impact.
5. Define treatment and estimate residual risk with rationale.
6. Escalate to authorized risk owner and screen DPIA/impact assessment needs.
7. Assign actions, owners, due dates, and verification evidence.
Illustrative likelihood: 1 Rare–5 Almost certain. Impact: 1 Negligible–5 Severe. Bands: 1–4 Low, 5–9 Moderate, 10–16 High, 17–25 Critical. These are not ISO-prescribed thresholds. Consider severity, vulnerable people, scale, sensitive data, monitoring, novel technology, and legal requirements. A register does not replace a legally required DPIA.
